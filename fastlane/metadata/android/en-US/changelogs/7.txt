Version 1.2.3

- 2D charts now follow live data while zoomed in — the view keeps up with new packets instead of drifting onto old or empty time.
- Smoother, steadier 3D-map clustering — points no longer flicker as you move the camera, and more show when zoomed into sparse areas.
- Received Packets columns are ordered by recent activity, so the most active repeaters lead.
- CSV export now shows the saved file's size.

Fixed: CSV export/import now preserves messages with line breaks; the Received Packets table no longer clears when opening some columns; the "load previously captured data?" prompt reliably appears on reopen.
