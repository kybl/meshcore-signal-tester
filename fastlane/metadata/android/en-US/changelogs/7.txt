Version 1.3.0

- Packet positions can come from the connected MeshCore device's own GPS instead of the phone (car roof, dog collar…) — no phone location permission needed for that mode.
- The device's radio preset and node name are shown; battery level now shows on its own.
- Public-channel messages are decrypted; Trace packets show per-hop SNR; suspicious "zero-stuffed" frames are flagged.
- RSSI chart draws the radio's measured noise floor; dates are shown next to times that aren't from today.
- More persistent auto-reconnect on Android, with a "Pairing required" prompt; new per-packet knock-then-tone sound.
- 3D-map controls moved onto the map; various layout tweaks.

Fixed: packets that showed a "Path payload too short" error now decode; the packet table no longer scrolls back to the selected column on every packet; accidental chart/map resizing is less likely.

Updated to target Android 16.
