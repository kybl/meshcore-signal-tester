Version 1.2.0

- Zoomable and pannable 2D SNR & RSSI charts.
- The full capture history is now kept on disk and survives a reload or restart
  (you are asked whether to resume the previous session on launch).
- More 3D map styles (CARTO, Esri, extra OpenStreetMap flavours) and a
  "None (no map)" option.
- Longer history durations (3 h and 12 h).
- Android: fixed a freeze after running for hours and improved background
  recovery.
- Various light/dark readability fixes.
